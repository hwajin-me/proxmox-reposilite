Reproduce the complete suite from this directory:

  REPOSILITE_TEST_JAR=/absolute/path/reposilite-3.6.5-all.jar REPOSILITE_TEST_JAVA=/absolute/path/jdk-21/bin/java python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v

Without REPOSILITE_TEST_JAR the four optional JDBC checks are skipped. CI
downloads the official fixture and verifies its publisher-provided SHA256.
Local TLS tests require OpenSSL 3, as installed in Alpine; macOS's bundled
LibreSSL does not provide the required hostname-check options. On Homebrew,
prepend /opt/homebrew/opt/openssl@3/bin to PATH when running the suite.
